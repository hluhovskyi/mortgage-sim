Assumptions

Returns are a smooth yearly average. Each month, the cash not needed for the higher payment is invested; tax benefits are invested at year end. Selling costs and investment gains tax apply at the horizon.

Not modeled: market volatility and sequence of returns, PMI, closing costs and points, refinancing, inflation, liquidity (cash tied up in the house versus investments), and loan qualification or income limits.
